# Single-face Stephenson option

Alternative axial packaging, same solved motion. No load qualification for longer cantilever shafts; V-crank web and fastener manufacture require detail design.

Nominal clearance: FAIL. Conflicts: 2.

Both crank arms are coplanar and fixed to a single hub: fabricate as one V-shaped crank. The two connecting rods need separate layers; this is not a completely coplanar construction. The rocker and inner spar also share one plane and form one rigid assembly. Original synthesized lengths and mounting angles are unchanged. **Known failure: the shoulder rod crosses the extended elbow crank pin D on each wing. Do not fabricate this arrangement as shown.** Placing the elbow rod on the inside instead causes interference with the shaft or shoulder joint. Small axial-spacing changes alone do not resolve these observed conflicts; a different crank/pin support arrangement, spatial bracket design, or re-synthesis is needed. The stand, belt and reversing gears remain. Longer overhung shafts require a new load/deflection check before fabrication.
